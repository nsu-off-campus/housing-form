Unit Condition & Exit Survey

One form for your move-in condition report or your move-out check-out. It takes about 5 minutes. Have your phone camera ready for photos of your bedroom and any problems you find.

01You & your unit

Why are you completing this form?

Submit within 48 hours of moving in. Anything you don't report is considered clean, safe and in good condition, so note every defect, damage, safety or pest concern now.

Term
Housing location (check all that apply)

02Move-out checklist

Confirm each item before you leave the apartment.

03Unit condition

For each area, tap Issues to report only if something is damaged, dirty, missing, not working, or a safety or pest concern. Then tag the items and describe the problem.

04Photos

Add photos of your bedroom, the shared areas, and every issue you reported. They protect you when responsibility for damage is decided.

Take or choose photos JPG, PNG or HEIC · as many as you need

Photos stay on your phone until you email them. After you submit, you'll attach them to the email this form opens.

05Your housing experience

Do you have recommendations for improving the off-campus housing experience?

06Acknowledge & sign

Last step: send it by email

  1. Tap Open email. Your report is filled in and addressed to both offices.
  2. Attach your 0 photos in your mail app.
  3. Press send. Your form isn't submitted until the email goes out.
